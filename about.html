About QC Title

Built in Charlotte. Serving the Carolinas.

QC Title brings together experienced title service, responsive communication, and dependable protection for real estate transactions throughout North and South Carolina. Our Charlotte roots shape the way we work: professional, relationship-driven, and focused on helping every transaction move forward with confidence.

Who we are

Local perspective. Broader reach.

Real estate is personal. Whether the transaction involves a family home, a piece of land, or a commercial property, the details behind ownership matter. QC Title approaches every file with careful review, clear communication, and a commitment to protecting the people and property behind the transaction.

Our ownership group comes from the real estate industry. That background shows up in the small things: returning calls, explaining what a requirement actually means, and keeping every party informed so nobody is guessing about where the file stands.

How we work

Careful work. Clear communication.

The same standard applies to every file, whatever the property type or the price.

Thorough review

Ownership history, liens, easements, restrictions, and requirements are examined carefully before anything is insured.

Plain communication

Status updates you can read in a minute, and a real person to call when a question comes up.

Dependable protection

Title insurance policies underwritten by WFG National Title Insurance Company and Commonwealth Land Title Insurance Company.
