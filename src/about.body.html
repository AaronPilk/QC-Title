Built in Charlotte. Serving the Carolinas.

Who we are

Local perspective. Broader reach.

Real estate is personal. Whether the transaction involves a family home, a piece of land, or a commercial property, the details behind ownership matter. QC Title approaches every file with careful review, clear communication, and a commitment to protecting the people and property behind the transaction.

Our ownership group comes from the real estate industry. That background shows up in the small things: returning calls, explaining what a requirement actually means, and keeping every party informed so nobody is guessing about where the file stands.
